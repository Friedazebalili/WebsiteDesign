Overview

Systor's Hard Disk Drive (HDD) / Solid State Drive (SSD) Dual Port Duplicators are the best in their class. A complete standalone unit that can clone a 3.5" and 2.5" HDD/SSD to multiple hard disks in a single touch of a button. It copies the data sector by sector from the source drive to the target drives simultaneously, regardless of brand, operating system or format of the drives. The copy includes partition and boot sector information for a perfect clone.

Systor HDD/SSD Duplicators are also DoD compliant when it comes to sanitizing and erasing data in the hard drive. This feature ensures that there is no trace of content left behind, for the customer's security and peace of mind.
