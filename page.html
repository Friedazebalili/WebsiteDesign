About EZ Dupe

Over 30 years of duplication development.

EZ Dupe was founded in Los Angeles, California in 1995 to develop data duplication systems for the Americas. Today, the company serves customers across more than 112 countries and continues to develop dedicated duplication hardware for modern storage media.

Our history

From Los Angeles to a global duplication business.

EZ Dupe was founded in 1995 and expanded rapidly across the Americas. In 2005, the company established R&D and manufacturing facilities in Taiwan to improve product quality, flexibility and support for markets beyond the Americas.
