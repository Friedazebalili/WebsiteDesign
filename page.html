Making copies of digital media since 1997.

Systor Systems prides itself as one of the leading manufacturers and retailers of duplication equipment in the industry. Parts are sourced from all over the world, and every complete system is assembled and fully tested in the USA.

How the line grew

  1. 1997

    Portable storage

    The company began with sales of portable storage devices, such as external optical disc burners and hard drives.

  2. Next

    CD, DVD and Blu-ray duplication

    As the product line became more focused, the business developed expertise in CD DVD and Blu-ray duplication equipment, and all things related to creating and publishing media.

  3. Today

    Flash, hard drives and NVMe

    Prosumers demand versatility, so Systor expanded into devices that mass produce flash memory cards (USB, SD, CF) and hard drives: mechanical HDD, solid state SSD and NVMe.

Main products

  • M.2 NVMe PCIe Duplicator / Sanitizer
  • Hard Drive Duplicator / Sanitizer
  • Flash Media Duplicator
  • CD DVD BD Blu-ray Duplicator

What we stay committed to

We stay committed to high quality and reliability in our products, with experienced customer service and qualified technical support for everyone involved in all types of media production.

That is why each tower ships with self diagnostics, a read-only source port, and a pass or fail result for every drive it copies or erases.

Government and school orders

eSystor will gladly accept government and school purchase orders. Since our company's inception, we have accepted purchase orders from city, state and federal government agencies, and from academic institutions, both public and private.
