Versatile, reliable duplication for prosumers.

Producers and consumers alike demand versatility and innovation in publishing equipment. That is why Systor makes devices that mass produce every common form of digital media: CD, DVD and Blu-ray discs, flash memory cards (USB, SD, CF), and hard drives, from mechanical HDD to solid state SSD and NVMe.

Our duplicator parts and components are sourced from all over the world, but every complete system is assembled and fully tested in the USA. We stay committed to high quality and reliability, with experienced customer service and qualified technical support for everyone involved in media production.

How the line grew

From one external burner to four product families.

  1. 1997

    Portable storage

    Systor starts out selling portable storage devices, such as external optical disc burners and hard drives.

  2. Next

    CD, DVD and Blu-ray

    The line focuses on disc duplication equipment and everything related to creating and publishing media.

  3. Today

    Flash, HDD, SSD and NVMe

    Duplicators and DoD sanitizers for USB, SD, CF, hard drives, SSDs and M.2 NVMe, in tower, flatbed and rackmount builds.

How every Systor is made

Each unit follows the same four steps before it ships.

  1. 01

    Sourced worldwide

    Duplicator parts and components come from trusted suppliers around the world.

  2. 02

    Assembled in the USA

    Every complete system is built at our facility in Walnut, California.

  3. 03

    Fully tested

    Each unit is tested before shipping, and runs its own self diagnostics at every power on.

  4. 04

    Supported for years

    3 years labor and 1 year parts on tower duplicators, with technical support by phone and email.
